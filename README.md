## 🪷 About BODHESSIA

**BODHESSIA** is an independent digital knowledge platform being created to make
Buddhist history, philosophy, traditions and cultural heritage accessible through
a modern, immersive and visually rich experience.

The long-term vision is to build a comprehensive digital archive covering:

```text
BUDDHAS
    ↓
BODHISATTVAS
    ↓
TEACHINGS
    ↓
SYMBOLS
    ↓
HISTORY
    ↓
TRADITIONS
    ↓
TEXTS
    ↓
SACRED PLACES
    ↓
ART & ARCHITECTURE
````

BODHESSIA is designed to bring together information from different Buddhist
traditions while clearly distinguishing historical evidence, canonical sources,
traditional accounts and scholarly interpretation.

## 🖋 Typography

BODHESSIA uses a typography system based on contrast.

```text
DISPLAY
Elegant editorial serif

BODY
Modern readable sans-serif

METADATA
Small uppercase typography
with generous letter spacing
```

Typography is treated as a primary visual element rather than simply
a method of displaying information.

## 🔬 Editorial Principles

BODHESSIA aims to present Buddhist knowledge responsibly.

Where appropriate, content will distinguish between:

```text
HISTORICAL EVIDENCE

ARCHAEOLOGICAL EVIDENCE

CANONICAL SOURCES

TRADITIONAL ACCOUNTS

LATER COMMENTARY

SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION
```

Different Buddhist traditions may preserve different understandings of
the same subject.

BODHESSIA therefore avoids presenting one tradition's interpretation as
the universal Buddhist position.
